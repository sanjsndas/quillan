Cookie Policy

How we use cookies to cultivate a better reading experience. Transparency, choice, and a pinch of simplicity.

Last updated: 22 July 2026

At Quillan Cookie Meadow, we believe in clarity. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them on our website, and how you can manage them. Our approach is straightforward: we use minimal, essential cookies to keep the site running smoothly and to understand how our readers engage with our content — nothing more.

📌 In short: We use only necessary and performance cookies. We do not sell your data, and we never use invasive tracking. You are in control.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners. Think of them as a short-term memory for your browser.

How we use cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience on Quillan Cookie Meadow. Our use falls into the following categories:

TypePurposeExamples
Strictly NecessaryThese are essential for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation and access to secure areas.Session ID, security tokens
Performance / AnalyticsWe use these to understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., which articles are most popular). This helps us improve our content and design.Pageview counts, time on page (via privacy-friendly analytics)
PreferenceThese cookies allow the site to remember choices you have made (like your preferred language or reading settings) to provide a more personalized experience.Language preferences, theme settings

We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Your reading habits are your own.

Managing your cookie preferences

You can control and manage cookies in various ways. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. To do this, please refer to the settings in your specific browser (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari). However, please note that blocking essential cookies may affect the functionality of the site.

  • Browser settings: You can set your browser to refuse all or some browser cookies, or to alert you when websites set or access cookies.
  • Opt-out tools: For analytics, we use privacy-first tools that respect Do-Not-Track signals. You can also disable JavaScript in your browser if you prefer.

For more detailed information about cookies and how to manage them, visit www.aboutcookies.org.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
