Privacy &
protection

We believe your data is yours. Read how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

✧ last updated: july 2026 📬 12 Meadow Lane 🛡️ GDPR compliant

Information we collect

We collect minimal data to maintain the journal and improve your experience. This includes:

  • Contact information — if you reach out via email or the contact form, we store your name, email address, and message for the purpose of responding.
  • Usage data — anonymized analytics (page views, referral sources) to understand what our readers enjoy. We never track individuals.
  • Cookies — we use a single session cookie to remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time.

✧ Our promise: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data stays within the Meadow.

How we use your information

Your information helps us:

  • Respond to inquiries and editorial pitches.
  • Improve our content and website performance.
  • Send occasional updates (only if you opt in).

Data security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS) and store data on secure servers. Access is restricted to editorial staff who need it.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time. Email us at privacy@quillancookie.meadow.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, except for essential services (hosting, email). These partners are GDPR-compliant.

Children’s privacy

Our content is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Cookies & tracking

We use a single functional cookie to remember your theme preference. No tracking cookies are placed without your explicit consent. You can manage cookie settings in your browser.

For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
